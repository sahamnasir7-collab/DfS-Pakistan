About the provider

Where textile research meets the factory floor

The National Textile Research Centre (NTRC) operates under National Textile University — Pakistan's premier institution for textile education, research and industrial innovation. It is a leading industrial research, testing, consultancy and capacity-development centre for the textile and apparel sector.

NTRC maintains an ISO/IEC 17025 accredited laboratory and works closely with manufacturers, exporters, development organisations and government agencies to improve product quality, environmental sustainability, resource efficiency and global competitiveness.

Training topics

What NTRC delivers under DfS

Water Stewardship

Water efficiency, water footprint, conservation practices and best industrial practices.

Wastewater Management

Effluent characterization, treatment technologies, compliance requirements and optimization.

Chemical Management

Sustainable chemical selection, MRSL/RSL, chemical inventory management and risk reduction.

ZDHC Implementation

ZDHC framework, wastewater requirements, MRSL implementation and supplier engagement.

Life Cycle Assessment (LCA)

Environmental impact assessment across the textile product lifecycle and interpretation of LCA results.

Digital Product Passport & Traceability

Supply-chain transparency, traceability systems, DPP concepts and emerging EU sustainability requirements.
