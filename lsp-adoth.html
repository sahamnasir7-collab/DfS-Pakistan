About the provider

Sustainability is a dialogue, not a directive

AdotH Management Consultants is a Karachi-based firm delivering ISO standards, management advisory, organizational development and professional training — with a cost-effective focus on helping SMEs prosper. Its DfS approach is people-centred: fostering sustainable change across social and economic dimensions by placing people at the centre of every transformation.

AdotH facilitates structured dialogue between management, middle management and factory workers — using Change Management Team (CMT) workshops, root-cause analysis and action plans to build trust, align teams and embed a culture of continuous improvement.
