About the provider

A management consulting firm & capacity-building institute

BTIL Academy is a management consulting firm and capacity-building institute, founded in 2018 to support social activists, civil society leaders, and development and humanitarian organizations in fulfilling their goals and vision. Driven by a passion for character ethics, BTIL supports its clients in building the capacity to become better leaders, build more sustainable organizations, and implement impactful, lasting change.

The institute describes its overall approach around three principles: dedication to work it is genuinely committed to, creativity rather than a fixed formula, and continuous learning — of best practice, of each client's field, and of new approaches to development work.

The provider's lead consultant

Led by an organizational development & change specialist

Momina Dar is an Organizational Development & Change Management Consultant, HR Strategist and Certified Leadership Coach with over 20 years of experience across organizational development, human resources and institutional transformation. She partners with organizations to strengthen leadership, build people-centred systems and drive sustainable organizational change.

Her expertise spans international development, textile and manufacturing, government, academia and the corporate sector — leading complex organizational assessments and designing HR strategies, compensation and performance management systems, grievance and accountability mechanisms, and organizational policies.

As a skilled facilitator and certified leadership coach, she has designed and delivered more than 100 highly participatory workshops covering leadership, organizational culture, change management, dialogue and stakeholder engagement, performance management, conflict resolution, grievance handling, diversity, equity and inclusion (DEI), and Training of Trainers (ToT).

She is particularly recognized for facilitating organization-wide transformation initiatives that build internal ownership rather than dependency — emphasizing collaborative problem-solving, cross-functional dialogue and coaching of change management teams so improvement continues long after external support concludes.

Included at no additional cost

A customized HR Policy & Procedure Manual

Where an organization already has an HR manual or documented policies, BTIL reviews and improves it — filling gaps, updating language and aligning it with good practice. Where none exists, BTIL builds one from scratch, drawing on what's learned through the programme. It covers recruitment and onboarding, attendance and leave management, grievance handling, disciplinary procedures and worker communication — a lasting resource that outlives the four months.

Methodology

  • Stakeholder mapping & dialogue facilitation
  • Problem tree analysis & five whys root-cause diagnosis
  • Fishbone analysis for problem-solving
  • Change Management Team formation & coaching
  • Consultant visits with progress review & coaching sessions
  • Remote check-ins & troubleshooting support between visits

Expected outcomes

  • One clearly defined organizational challenge with a root-cause map
  • Baseline assessment and 2–3 measurable success indicators
  • Tested interventions with gathered feedback
  • Strengthened dialogue mechanisms and ownership
  • A documented Sustainability Action Plan and DfS case study
