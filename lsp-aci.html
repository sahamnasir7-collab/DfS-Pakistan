About the provider

A productivity partner for Pakistan's exporters.

The Asian Competitiveness Institute (ACI) helps public and private organisations achieve sustainability by improving processes, lifting performance and building internal capacity.

ACI works across consulting, training, research, financial advocacy and ERP solutions, with deep specialisation in Lean manufacturing and Kaizen. As a GIZ Local Service Provider, it turns the Dialogue for Sustainability framework into concrete shop-floor results.

ACI's signature method · O³D

One Organization, One Department

Pinpoint the single weakest department, fix it end-to-end, and build a model line the rest of the organisation can follow.

Salient features

What makes the programme work?

Certified DfS Practitioner

Full completion earns a recognised joint ACI–GIZ credential — not just an attendance record.

A dedicated engineer on-site

At least one Industrial Engineer per company for pre-assessment, live assessment and follow-up.

The six-step DfS method

From problem identification through cause and goal analysis to a fully costed action plan.

Mini-CMT follow-through

Action plans are carried out, with progress tracked so that the savings are actually realised.

Recognition & model status

Become a Model DfS Company, win the GIZ DfS Productivity Award, and qualify for the EU–GIZ Challenge Fund.

Standardised toolkit

Templates for data, analysis and reporting — with all materials in hard and soft copy.
