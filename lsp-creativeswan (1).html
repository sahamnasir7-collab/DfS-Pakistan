About the provider

Management & process consulting, built on scale experience

CreativeSwan is a management and process consulting firm offering consulting, capacity-building trainings and one-on-one coaching — spanning DEI initiatives, business process re-engineering, energy studies and behaviour-based safety, alongside its core change management work.

The firm has worked across banking, energy, fertilizer, manufacturing, ports and public-sector clients, bringing structured, research-based methodology rather than one-size-fits-all templates to each engagement. CreativeSwan has also worked directly with GIZ before, serving as National Master Trainer, Consultant and Coach on a related capacity-building programme for environmental consultants.

Areas of work

What CreativeSwan delivers under DfS

Grouped into three pillars, drawn directly from the programme's own scope — led by the one CreativeSwan's own portfolio evidences most strongly.

01

Business Process & Operational Excellence

Productivity enhancement, cost reduction and process improvement — remapping inefficient, ad-hoc processes into streamlined, measurable ones. This is the anchor of CreativeSwan's change management work, and the engine behind the 12-week programme's baseline-KPI approach.

  • Productivity enhancement, cost reduction & process improvement
  • Efficient plant management
  • Maintenance management & electrical safety (NFPA 70E)
02

Organizational Transformation & People

Building the human and structural side of change alongside the process work.

  • Strategic HR management
  • Organization development — culture, talent & learning
  • Building diversity (including gender)
  • Training needs assessment (TNA)
03

Strategy & Growth

Positioning the organization for what comes after the immediate fix.

  • International expansion & overseas client relationships
  • Project feasibility & project management
  • Innovation & creativity

Implementation roadmap

A focused, hybrid 12-week engagement

A hybrid engagement: one 2-day, face-to-face workshop sets the framework, followed by structured virtual check-ins as the team leads the work itself — 6 days of direct engagement across the 12 weeks.
W1
Week 1 · In-person

Kick-off Workshop

2-day face-to-face workshop — CreativeSwan leads, providing the improvement framework to the team.
W2
Week 2

Initiative Finalized

The team finalizes its chosen initiative/project after discussion with its own leadership.
W3
Week 3

Action Plan & Baseline KPIs

The team presents its action plan, including baseline KPIs for measuring progress; CreativeSwan provides feedback.
→
Weeks 4–5 — the team continues the work independently
W6
Week 6

Progress Review #1

The team presents its progress; CreativeSwan reviews and provides feedback.
→
Weeks 7–8 — the team continues the work
W9
Week 9

Progress Review #2

A second progress review — CreativeSwan reviews and provides feedback.
→
Weeks 10–11 — the team continues the work
W12
Week 12

Final Results

The team presents final results, including improvements measured against the baseline KPIs — CreativeSwan reviews and provides final feedback.
