About the provider

University-backed capacity building

NED Academy is the professional training and capacity-development arm of NED University of Engineering & Technology, Karachi — the pivotal wing of the university's 100-year legacy, with over two decades of professional capacity building and modern facilities, laboratories and multimedia resources.

Its DfS engagement pairs organisational change management with technical capacity building, backed by NED University's multidisciplinary experts, to help SMEs adopt sustainable, efficient and future-ready practices.

Technical focus areas

What NED delivers under DfS

Energy Efficiency & Management

Energy-saving opportunities, utility performance monitoring, energy conservation and cost optimisation.

Resource Efficiency & Cleaner Production

Material-wastage reduction, process optimisation, lean manufacturing and resource productivity.

Water Conservation & Wastewater

Water-consumption assessment, water-saving practices and sustainable wastewater management.

ESG & Sustainability Readiness

ESG principles, compliance requirements, international buyer expectations and sustainability reporting.

Productivity through Sustainable Practices

Continuous-improvement culture, employee engagement and sustainable operational excellence.

Diagnostics & Digital Monitoring

Sustainability diagnostic reports, ESG readiness assessment and online monitoring tools.

Sustainability roadmap

A physical + remote model — 4 on-site visits and 6 bi-weekly remote mentoring sessions — with baseline, mid-term and final assessments and a sustainability roadmap. Travel, boarding and lodging outside the base city are arranged by the participating company, or as mutually agreed.

Expected Outcomes & KPIs

  • Functional Change Management Team established
  • Customised sustainability action plan implemented
  • 5–15 sustainability improvement actions identified & initiated
  • Energy & resource-efficiency opportunities documented
  • Stronger ESG & sustainability awareness
  • Sustainability roadmap for continuous improvement
