About the provider

Turning HREDD from compliance into a business advantage

In today's global business environment, buyers, investors, regulators and international supply chains increasingly expect businesses to demonstrate responsible, transparent and sustainable operations. Human Rights & Environmental Due Diligence (HREDD) is no longer only a compliance requirement. It has become a strategic business advantage.

InterAct Consulting supports SMEs in establishing a practical HREDD management system, designed specifically for Pakistan-based SMEs with a focus on practicality, affordability, operational efficiency, export readiness and long-term business resilience.

International markets, especially in Europe and North America, increasingly require suppliers to demonstrate responsible business conduct. SMEs implementing HREDD gain a real competitive advantage, including improved access to international buyers, reduced legal and reputational risk, and stronger relationships with workers, regulators and supply-chain partners.

Value proposition

What the SME will gain

The investment in HREDD implementation helps the SME

  • Strengthen compliance with international expectations

  • Build long-term sustainability and competitiveness
  • Prepare for future ESG and due diligence requirements
Scope of services

What InterAct delivers under DfS

Baseline Assessment & Gap Analysis

Review of current practices, policies, systems and compliance status against HREDD principles and international expectations.

Human Rights & Environmental Risk Assessment

Identifying actual and potential human rights and environmental risks across operations and the supply chain.

Stakeholder Consultation

Meetings with management, workers and relevant stakeholders, prioritising vulnerable groups and high-risk areas.

HREDD Policy Development

Drafting the SME's formal HREDD policy and procedures, setting out its commitment to human rights and environmental responsibility.

Governance Structure Support

Defining HREDD responsibilities and establishing an internal committee or focal team with clear reporting lines.

Grievance Mechanism Design

A worker complaint system with anonymous reporting channels and a clear investigation and resolution process.

Capacity Building & Training

Management training and worker awareness sessions covering HREDD principles, rights and the grievance mechanism.

Monitoring, Mitigation & Reporting

KPI-based monitoring, corrective action planning, and a reporting framework for ongoing HREDD performance.
