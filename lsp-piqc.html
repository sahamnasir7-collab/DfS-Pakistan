About the provider

A center for excellence in quality

PIQC Institute of Quality is a long-established quality and productivity institute with campuses in Lahore and Karachi, delivering training and consulting in Total Quality Management, Lean, Six Sigma, 5S, Kaizen, QCCs, ISO standards and Dialogue for Sustainability. Its instructors are highly qualified and practically experienced in industry.

PIQC delivers the Dialogue for Sustainability programme — GIZ's Quality & Productivity Improvement initiative, first launched in Pakistan's textile sector in 2014 — as a structured four-month professional diploma (Certified DfS Professional, CDfSP) that combines classroom learning with real implementation on the factory floor.

Course contents

What the programme covers

DfS Methodology & Six Steps

Introduction to GIZ's DfS, the Six Steps of DfS, and the critical success factors for implementation.

Change Management Teams

Building CMTs and mini-CMTs, vertical integration of teams across management, supervisors and workers, and gap analysis.

Productivity Improvement Tools

Seven wastes, Lean management, line balancing & value stream mapping, Kaizen, Poka-yoke and 5S.

Quality Improvement Tools

Quality management fundamentals, KPIs & dashboards, process standardization, and Measurement System Analysis (MSA).

Lean Six Sigma & Problem Solving

Lean Six Sigma, cost of quality, statistical tools, Quality Circles, FMEA, Gemba Walks and data analytics.

Applied CMT Project

Real-life exercises, root-cause and goal analysis, action plans, performance KPIs and a final implementation report.
