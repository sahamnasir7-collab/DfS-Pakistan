About the provider

Pakistan's leading HSE & compliance consultancy

Grow Safe (Private) Limited (GSPL) is a leading compliance and management consultancy offering HSE consultancy, sustainability solutions, corporate training, management-system consultancy, third-party certifications, inspections, testing, monitoring, HR & ISO consultancy, technical supplies and engineering projects.

GSPL is certified to ISO 9001, 14001 & 45001 and DfS, with international accreditations via IAF (Italy) and EIAC (UAE). It is a member of KCCI, PEC and PEF, an Exemplar Global registered ISO Lead-Auditor training provider, and a human-capital-development partner of Ziauddin University, the University of Karachi and the Health Services Academy.

Assessment services

What GSPL delivers under DfS

Fire Safety Gaps & Risk Assessment

Fire hazard identification, prevention-system effectiveness and complete fire-safety solutions.

Chemical Risk (COSHH) Assessment

Chemical-exposure risks, safe handling & storage, and health & environmental impacts.

Hazard Area Zone Classification

Flammable-release sources, zone categories (0/1/2 & 20/21/22) and ignition-risk controls.

Electrical Safety Assessment

IR testing, thermographic inspection, load balancing and earth-resistance testing.

Fire Load Calculation

Combustible-material survey, calorific values and fire-load density & zone classification.

Energy Conservation Survey

Energy-consuming systems, efficiency evaluation, loss identification and savings recommendations.

LOTOTO / Maintenance Safety

Lock-out/tag-out, energy-isolation controls, Permit-to-Work and maintenance-safety practices.

Root Cause Analysis

Incident investigation, immediate/underlying/root causes, and corrective & preventive actions (CAPA).
